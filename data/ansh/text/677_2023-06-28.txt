                CEO  REPORT    TO  GLPTC   BOARD    OF DIRECTORS                    
                                                                                    
                                MEETING DATE: 6/28/2023                             
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          CEO                                                                       
          As you read through this report, you’ll see June has been a busy month. It is exciting to work
          with such engaged coworkers!                                              
                                                                                    
          We received one proposal for vanpool services from Commute with Enterprise. I am working
          with them on the procurement process we follow for a single proposal and will have a contract
          by the July Board meeting.                                                
                                                                                    
          We received the green light from the Attorney General’s office for the NEORide agreement and
          have officially joined the NEORide council of governments.                
                                                                                    
          In addition to contract discussions with Purdue for service this upcoming school year, we have
          been negotiating with Scion group, who manage the Lark, Alight, and Redpoint apartment
          complexes for service in the fall. We were also contacted by another management group for
          other apartment complexes in West Lafayette and have had a preliminary meeting to discuss
          possible service.                                                         
                                                                                    
          This all leads to budget season for CityBus. We must approve a budget at our August meeting
          to meet the September 1st deadline from the City of Lafayette. I will present the annual budget
          at the September 5th council meeting, there will be a public meeting October 5th, and final
          adoption by City Council on October 19th. It will be my goal to have a draft budget for review at
          the July board meeting, with a final vote in August.                      
                                                                                    
          FINANCE                                                                   
            •  Continue to work with Crowe on annual financial audit-should be done by board meeting.
            •  Continuing work on federal grant consolidation and closeout          
            •  Worked with FTA regional office to submit grant applications for lapsing 5339 grants.
            •  Negotiated with Avail on ERP system renewal and upgrade schedule. Cost reduced from
                                                                                    
               their initial proposal.                                              
            •  Finance audit of 2022 is being wrapped up. Two deficiencies found in the audit. One is
               related to incomplete procedure and/or lack of training due to finance team turnover in
               2021 and 2022. The other is related to process change of payroll system to PayCom.
               The team is working on amending procedures to address audit findings.
                                                                                    
          HUMAN  RESOURCES                                                          
            •  HR has onboarded six (6) new full-time Bus Operators who are currently in training.
            •  We’ve hired a Payroll & Benefits Specialist, two Executive Assistants/Project Managers,
               and a PM Street Supervisor                                           
            •  We are currently accepting applications for A,B, and C Level Mechanics.
            •  Contracts for third party administrator for the FTA Drug & Alcohol Program, FMLA, and
               ADA have been completed and are slated to begin 7-1-23               
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          MAINTENANCE                                                               
            •  Engine Rebuild for 4001 Complete.                                    
            •  Engine repairs for 1701 complete.                                    
            •  Accident repairs underway for bus 4012.                              
            •  New Ram Pro-masters arrived and work in progress for electronics for service.
                                                                                    
          OPERATIONS                                                                
            •  Operations is working with Training to accomplish Summer Refresher Training with all
               operators participating. This investment in operator skill and knowledge will assist us in
               accomplishing our retention goals.                                   
            •  Operations departmental reorganization continues with the posting of select positions.
               Vacant Dispatcher and Supervisor positions have been filled by internal promotion.
               Ensuring an adequate administrative staff is available to direct operators in delivering
               outstanding customer experience safely and efficiently is critical.  
                                                                                    
          CUSTOMER  EXPERIENCE                                                      
            •  Bus stop improvement project at six locations across the service area in underway with
               the installation of shelters and ADA accessible amenities in the coming months.
               Additional improvements are being planned at various locations including the CityBus
               Center.                                                              
            •  Service Agreement negotiations with various community partners are underway with the
               goal of providing the best possible transit services to all those we serve while being good
               stewards of available funding.

               PTASP

               - All 7 are now in the final phase of training - cadetting with drivers on route
            •  Developed comprehensive refresher training week for all operators    
               - Refresher training will run for 7 weeks                            
               - 15 Hours of driving instruction is included                        
               - Guest speaker, Charlie Vaught, Loss Control Expert with Gregory & Appel, will
                 present each Wednesday

                 challenging event to promote professional driving in a close quarter environment
