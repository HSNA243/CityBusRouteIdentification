                                      Fare Policy                                   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          I. Purpose                                                                
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          The CityBus Fare Policy provides a strategic framework for fare setting and collection,
          ensuring equitable, efficient, and financially responsible transit services. Guided by
          CityBus’ mission, this policy supports safe, reliable, and sustainable mobility while
          fostering accessibility and connectivity. By balancing affordability with financial
                                                                                    
          stewardship, CityBus aims to enhance the quality of life in our community while
          maintaining operational efficiency and embracing innovation.              
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          II. Policy Principles                                                     
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          CityBus Fare Policy is guided by the following principles:                
                                                                                    
          1. Equity & Accessibility: Ensure fares are structured to promote affordability and
             accessibility for all riders, particularly low-income individuals, seniors, and persons
             with differing abilities, in accordance with Title VI protections.     
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          2. Community-Centered Mobility: Support connectivity and provide mobility 
             services that meet the diverse needs of our community.                 
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          3. Financial Sustainability: Establish fares that contribute to operational funding
             while balancing affordability and long-term fiscal responsibility.     
                                                                                    
          4. Operational Efficiency: Maintain a fare collection system that minimizes
             administrative costs and improves ease of use for riders.              
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          5. Technological Integration: Adopt modern fare collection and payment systems
             technology to streamline access and improve customer experience.       
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          III. Board Oversight & Fare  Adjustments                                  
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          The CityBus Board of Directors has the authority to set the base fare, establish pass and
          discount programs, and define all aspects of the agency’s fare policy. Adjustments are
          made as needed to enhance operational efficiency, ensure financial sustainability, and
                                                                                    
          comply with the CityBus Title VI Program, which guarantees non-discriminatory access
          to transit services.                                                      
                                                                                    
         Page | 2

                                      Fare Policy                                   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          The Board oversees fare adjustments in alignment with the agency's strategic goals.
          Evaluations are based on:                                                 
                                                                                    
            1. Community mobility needs and regional transit integration.           
            2. Cost recovery goals and operational funding sustainability, with farebox recovery
                                                                                    
               ratio as a key benchmark.                                            
            3. Equity considerations, including impacts on minority and low-income populations,
               in compliance with Title VI requirements.                            
            4. Technological advancements in fare collection.                       
                                                                                    
            5. Industry best practices and peer agency comparisons.                 
                                                                                    
          Fare policies are reviewed periodically to ensure alignment with these priorities. Any
          changes must adhere to established policy principles and include a Fare Equity Analysis
          when required under Title VI regulations.                                 
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          IV. Commitment   to Public Engagement    & Transparency                   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          CityBus is committed to engaging the public in fare policy decisions through outreach
          efforts, stakeholder collaboration, and accessible communication. Fare changes will be
                                                                                    
          implemented transparently, with a clear timeline and rationale provided to the public and
          community partners. Public participation will be conducted in accordance with the CityBus
          Title VI Program to ensure meaningful engagement, particularly for minority and low-
          income populations.                                                       
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          V. Policy Implementation  & Review                                        
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          CityBus staff are responsible for implementing fare policies in accordance with the
          strategic framework set by the Board. Regular assessments of fare revenue, farebox
                                                                                    
          recovery ratio, and ridership trends will guide operational decisions. Policy updates will
          be made as necessary to align with financial sustainability and community mobility needs
          while ensuring compliance with Title VI non-discrimination requirements.

     TO:       Board of Directors                                                   
                                                                                    
     FROM:     Bryan T. Walck, Chief Operating Officer                              
                                                                                    
     DATE:     April 21, 2025                                                       
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     RE:       Fare Structure Analysis & Recommendation for Public Hearing          
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     As part of our comprehensive operations analysis (COA), RLS & Associates has conducted a Fare
     Structure Analysis to support our ongoing efforts. This analysis evaluated fare trends, peer system
     comparisons, and projected impacts on ridership and revenue under various fare change scenarios.
     CityBus staff concurs with RLS’s recommendation and supports advancing the proposed
                                                                                    
     Recommended Fare Structure for public input and adoption.                      
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     Background  and  Need  for Change                                              
                                                                                    
     CityBus has not adjusted fares in over 20 years, despite steadily rising operating costs. Since 2018,
     our operating cost per passenger trip has increased by 50 percent, while fare revenue has remained
     flat. Without corrective action, this growing gap will contribute to a budget shortfall that threatens
     agency stability and the continuity of service.                                
                                                                                    
     Although CityBus maintains one of the stronger farebox recovery ratios among peer systems (thanks
                                                                                    
     to prudent cost control and contract service revenue) ongoing cost pressures can no longer be
     absorbed without a fare adjustment.                                            
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     Key Features  of the Recommended   Fare  Structure                             
                                                                                    
       •  Base fare restructured as a two-hour window fare                          
          Riders purchase a single fare and gain unlimited boardings within a two-hour window from
          activation. This replaces the legacy one-way fare plus paper transfer and allows seamless
          connections or short round trips without an extra fare.                   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       •  Expanded half-fare eligibility                                            
          Half fare remains available to seniors (65 and older), Medicare recipients, and people with
          differing abilities. It will be extended to youth ages 9 to 19 who are enrolled in school, replacing
          the current free fare for that age group and ensuring consistent affordability for students.
                                                                                    
       •  Launch of Masabi’s EZFare mobile fare platform                            
          In August 2025, CityBus, through NEORide, will implement EZFare, Masabi’s 
          Justride-powered, account-based ticketing (ATB) fare system. Riders will use a branded
     GLPTC April Board Meeting Board Packet Pg.8                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                        Exhibit 2   
          mobile app or reloadable smartcard to pay as they go. Fare capping will ensure riders
          automatically receive the best value (1-Day or 31-Day) based on actual use, removing the
          need to purchase passes in advance and modernizing our fare collection processes.
                                                                                    
       •  Unlimited ride passes                                                     
          Unlimited ride passes will be adjusted to including 1-Day and 31-Day at both standard and
          half-fare prices, and 6-Month passes. Half Fare 1-Day passes are a new offering and 6-Month
          passes (January through June, and July through December) will replace current semester
                                                                                    
          passes offering year-round coverage over academic-year coverage.          
                                                                                    
       •  Tokens and transfers eliminated                                           
          We will discontinue physical tokens and paper transfers in favor of the two-hour window fare
          model and digital fare media.                                             
                                                                                    
       •  ACCESS fare pilot                                                         
          ACCESS paratransit fares will remain at their current level ($2.00 per one-way trip) for a
          six-month pilot beginning in January 2026. During this period, we will assess ridership and
          demand impacts with the goal of evaluating whether aligning fixed-route and paratransit fares
          at the same price point better advances equity and accessibility across service modes.
                                                                                    
       •  Express route fare                                                        
          Express route fare will be established at a rate that is closer to CityBus’ per-trip cost and will
          be paid by the general public while riding express routes (affiliated riders will continue to ride
          express routes fare free with proper credential). Express route fare will follow the two-hour fare
          window model but will only be valid on express routes. Regular route fares and passes will not
                                                                                    
          be accepted on Express routes.

     I recommend the Board approve and advance the Recommended Fare Structure to public hearings

     RE:       Service Standards Policy                                             
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     As CityBus continues to navigate financial pressures and prepare for a sustainable future, the
     development of an updated Service Standards policy is essential to guide our operational decisions.
     We have been proactive in recognizing the need for a high-frequency network to better serve our
     community. I am pleased to share a proposed policy that will serve as a strategic framework for
     planning, evaluating, and delivering transit services.                         
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     These standards are grounded in our mission to provide safe, reliable, and accessible mobility while
     being responsible stewards of public resources. They align service planning with our values of equity,
     efficiency, and adaptability.                                                  
                                                                                    
     Purpose of the Service Standards                                               
     The attached service standards document outlines expectations across several key areas:
                                                                                    
       •  Fixed-route and demand-response service categories                        
       •  Service hours and frequencies                                             
       •  Infrastructure and accessibility                                          
       •  On-time performance targets                                               
                                                                                    
       •  Equity considerations                                                     
       •  Performance metrics and route evaluation criteria                         
                                                                                    
     These standards will guide CityBus operations and planning efforts, ensuring transparency and
     accountability while maintaining the flexibility to adapt to evolving needs.   
                                                                                    
     Service Allocation Framework                                                   
     To balance equity and efficiency in service planning, the revised model allocates:
                                                                                    
       •  Approximately three-quarters of annual service hours to Core Routes, which prioritize frequency,
          reliability, and system connectivity;                                     
       •  One-quarter to Local Routes, which ensure coverage in lower-density areas during peak
          demand periods.

          I. Introduction                                                           
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         CityBus is committed to providing safe, reliable, and efficient transit service to the Greater
         Lafayette community. These service standards establish a strategic framework for service
         planning and operations, ensuring equitable, efficient, and financially responsible transit
         services. Guided by CityBus’ mission, this policy supports safe, reliable, and sustainable
         mobility while fostering accessibility and connectivity. By balancing service coverage with
         financial stewardship, CityBus aims to enhance the quality of life in our community while
         maintaining operational efficiency and embracing innovation.               
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         Mission Statement                                                          
                                                                                    
         The mission of CityBus is to be a proactive community partner. The agency strives to
         improve the quality of life by operating safe, reliable, and environmentally friendly transit
         services and partnering in local economic development activities. With employees who
         take pride in their work, CityBus provides excellent customer service and offers efficient,
         convenient access to destinations throughout the Lafayette and West Lafayette
         community.                                                                 
                                                                                    
         Our Vision                                                                 
                                                                                    
         CityBus has an important role in making the Lafayette and West Lafayette community a
         better place to live, work, go to school, and visit. With safe, reliable, and convenient transit
         services, the agency will offer a viable transportation choice for residents, employees, and
         visitors.                                                                  
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            • The agency will be recognized as a valuable resource for the community.
            • The agency will provide high-frequency transit services in areas with greater
              densities.                                                            
            • The agency will operate efficiently, giving taxpayers the best service possible for
              their investment.                                                     
            • The agency will be a leader in transit-oriented development efforts.  
            • The agency will bring together developers, local governments, and planning
              agencies to address land use decisions that impact mobility.          
            • The agency will be a leader in bringing environmentally sound transit technologies
              to the Lafayette and West Lafayette community.

         II. Purpose                                                                
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         These service standards provide a structured framework for planning and operational
         decisions, ensuring CityBus services align with the agency’s mission, vision, and guiding
         principles. While this document establishes baseline service expectations, it cannot
         account for every operational scenario. Exceptions and variations should align with the
         overarching principles of accessibility, efficiency, equity, and financial responsibility.
                                                                                    
         CityBus remains committed to continuously evaluating and adapting services to meet
         evolving community needs.                                                  
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         III. Fixed-Route Service Categories                                        
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         CityBus fixed-route services are structured to balance network efficiency and access,
         ensuring that high-ridership areas receive frequent service while maintaining coverage for
         lower-density areas. This distribution does not include Express and Contract Routes,
         which are structured based on contractual agreement.                       
                                                                                    
         The agency operates three fixed-route service categories:                  
                                                                                    
            1. Core Routes: Approximately three-quarters of annual service hours will be
              allocated to Core Routes. These frequency routes form the backbone of the
              network and provide frequent and reliable service along key corridors, ensuring
              connectivity across the system.                                       
                 a. Weekdays: Every 20 minutes from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM, higher frequency
                                                                                    
                   during peak hours is preferable.                                 
                 b. Saturdays: Every 20 minutes from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM, with lower 
                   frequencies or restricted service hours on select routes during off-peak
                   hours based on operational considerations.                       
                                                                                    
            2. Local Routes: Approximately one-quarter of annual service hours will be allocated
              to Local Routes. These coverage routes provide extended service to additional
              areas not served by the core routes but operate only during peak hours to support
              higher-demand time periods.                                           
                 a. Weekdays: Every 30-60 minutes during peak hours only, from 6:00 AM to
                   10:00 AM and 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM, with lower frequencies or restricted
                   service hours on select routes based on operational considerations.
                                                                                    
            3. Express and Contract Routes: These routes are designed to meet the specific
              mobility needs of funding contractual partners, whether through express, loop, or
              local route configurations. These routes are intended to enhance the overall
              network, as provided in these standards.

          IV. Demand-Response     Services                                          
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         In addition to the fixed-route network, CityBus operates demand-response service to
         enhance mobility for our community.                                        
                                                                                    
         The agency operates two demand-response service categories:                
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            1. CityBus ACCESS: Paratransit service operating within three-quarters of a mile of
              fixed routes during equivalent service hours, this service ensures accessibility for
              differently abled riders, following certification processes.          
                                                                                    
            2. CityBus FLEX: Microtransit service operating as an on-demand transit option
              designed to serve designated geographic zones and time periods where fixed-route
              service is less practical.                                            
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          V. Bus Stops &  Infrastructure                                            
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         CityBus optimizes bus stop placement to balance accessibility, convenience, and service
         efficiency. Stops are generally spaced between one-eighth and one-half mile, with closer
         spacing in high-density areas and more distance between stops in lower-density areas to
         improve travel times while maintaining accessibility.                      
                                                                                    
         CityBus prioritizes ADA compliance at as many stops as possible and is committed to
         enhancing accessibility systemwide. Shelters, benches, and other amenities are placed at
                                                                                    
         high-ridership locations where they provide the greatest benefit to the most riders, with
         additional investments made in partnership with private entities.          
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          VI. On-Time   Performance  &  Reliability                                 
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         CityBus is committed to delivering dependable transit services, maintaining an on-time
         performance standard of 85% for fixed routes, with arrivals no more than five minutes late
         and no early departures. Schedule adherence is regularly evaluated to accommodate
         travel times, passenger demand, and external conditions, ensuring operational
         consistency and reliability.

         VII. Accessibility & Equity                                                
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         CityBus ensures equitable transit access through ADA-compliant vehicles, ensuring all
         riders have access to safe and reliable transportation. Service modifications undergo Title
         VI analysis to assess potential impacts on minority and low-income populations,
         maintaining compliance with non-discrimination policies. Community engagement efforts
         prioritize outreach to underserved populations, incorporating public feedback into service
                                                                                    
         planning to align with diverse mobility needs.                             
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         VIII. Performance  Metrics & Evaluation                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         Service performance is measured through ridership productivity, cost efficiency, and
         customer feedback. Routes are evaluated based on:                          
                                                                                    
            1. Passengers per revenue hour to assess route productivity.            
                                                                                    
            2. Cost per trip to measure financial efficiency.                       
                                                                                    
            3. Overall service utilization to ensure resources are allocated effectively.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         IX. Conclusion                                                             
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         These service standards provide a structured approach to optimizing CityBus operations
         while ensuring equitable, efficient, and high-quality transit for the Greater Lafayette
         community. CityBus remains committed to delivering safe, reliable, and accessible transit
         services that enhance community mobility, connectivity, and economic vitality.
