     TO:       GLPTC Board of Directors                                             
                                                                                    
     FROM:     Joanne Zhang                                                         
                                                                                    
     DATE:     7/15/2025                                                            
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     RE:       2025 Low/No Grant Application                                        
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     The fiscal year 2025 competitive low/no emission grant application has been submitted on July 14.
                                                                                    
     Under this project, CityBus propose to deploy federal funds to modernize its fleet, perform critical facility
     refurbishments, and produce affordable hydrogen fuel. CityBus is requesting funds to replace 12 buses
     that have reached the end of their useful lives with 6 latest-generation CNG-powered buses and 6
     hydrogen fuel cell buses. Additionally, CityBus is requesting funds for a critical project to replace the
     faltering roof of the agency’s maintenance facility and to add solar panels to enable the agency to
     reduce its operating costs and boost its resiliency. Finally, CityBus is requesting support to deploy
     equipment that will utilize an existing, market-supplied commodity feedstock, methanol, to produce
     hydrogen fuel that is cost competitive with diesel. This will enable CityBus to reduce the operating costs
     of its hydrogen bus fleet by two-thirds and create a resilient supply of hydrogen fuel that is shielded
     from the price fluctuations of the developing hydrogen market.                 
                                                                                    
     The total federal grant requested is $23,808,100, which may be scaled down to a minimum of
     $17,151,100, with reduced number of buses and solar roof eliminated.           
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     In this proposal, we will partner with CTE, New Flyer of America, IVY Tech, DLZ, IVYS Energy
     Solutions, and Total Hydrogen Solutions.                                       
                                                                                    
     The result of the applications will be announced in Sep 2025.

           GREATER   LAFAYETTE   PUBLIC  TRANSPORTATION    CORPORATION              
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                 RESOLUTION   #25-02                                
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
           RESOLUTION  AUTHORIZING   FREE  FARES FOR  THE INITIAL LAUNCH            
               PERIOD  OF THE REIMAGINE   CITYBUS  SERVICE REDESIGN                 
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
               WHEREAS, CityBus is committed to providing safe, reliable, and accessible public
          transportation to the residents and visitors of Greater Lafayette; and    
                                                                                    
               WHEREAS, CityBus has undertaken a comprehensive system redesign, known as
          Reimagine CityBus, to improve service frequency, connectivity, and convenience through
                                                                                    
          new transfer points and route structures; and                             
                                                                                    
               WHEREAS  the successful launch of the redesigned service is critical to ensuring
          a smooth transition for current riders and encouraging new ridership; and 
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
               WHEREAS, offering free fares during the initial launch period will reduce barriers
          to access, promote public awareness, and allow riders to explore the new system without
          financial burden;                                                         
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
             NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of Directors of CityBus as 
                                       follows:                                     
                                                                                    
               1. Free Fare Period: CityBus shall implement a free-fare policy for all fixed-route
                                                                                    
               services for a period of two (2) weeks from August 11, 2025, through August 23,
               2025. The standard fare policy shall resume August 24, 2025.         
                                                                                    
               2. Public Communication: CityBus staff is directed to incorporate this free-fare
               period into the broader marketing and public outreach campaign to ensure
                                                                                    
               widespread awareness and participation.                              
                                                                                    
               3. Evaluation and Reporting: CityBus staff shall monitor ridership and community
               feedback during the free-fare period and provide a summary report to the Board.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          The designation is effective immediately and shall continue until specifically revoked.
          Any and all resolutions which conflict with this resolution are hereby rescinded. Adopted

          Agreement for a Transit Hub with Daycare and Housing Project              
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          This Agreement for a Transit Hub with Daycare and Housing Project (“Agreement”)
          is made and entered into on this ___ day of ____________, 2025, by and between:
                                                                                    
            •  IVY Tech Community College – Lafayette Campus, located at 3101 S. Creasy
               Ln., Lafayette, IN. 47905 ("IVY Tech"),                              
                                                                                    
            •  YMCA of Lafayette Indiana, located at 3001 S Creasy Ln., Lafayette, IN. 47905
               ("YMCA"), and                                                        
                                                                                    
            •  Greater Lafayette Public Transportation Corporation (CityBus), located at
               1250 Canal Rd., Lafayette, IN. 47904 ("CityBus"),                    
                                                                                    
          collectively referred to as the “Parties.”                                
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          1. Purpose                                                                
          This Agreement (“Agreement”) sets forth the terms and understanding between YMCA
                                                                                    
          of Greater Lafayette (“YMCA”), Ivy Tech Community College – Lafayette Campus (“Ivy
          Tech”), and the Greater Lafayette Public Transportation Corporation (“CityBus”)
          (collectively, “the Parties”) regarding a joint initiative to explore, fund, and construct a
          transportation hub, childcare, and workforce training facilities in the designated
          area between IVY Tech and the YMCA as shown in Exhibit A.                 
                                                                                    
          2. Project Overview                                                       
                                                                                    
          The project shall be completed in three stages:                           
                                                                                    
          Stage 1: Feasibility Study                                                
          A comprehensive feasibility study to assess site viability, community impact, zoning
          requirements, design concepts, cost projections, sustainability, potential risks, and
                                                                                    
          legalities.                                                               
                                                                                    
          Stage 2: Funding Sourcing                                                 
          Securing federal, state, and local funds, grants, private investment, and philanthropic
          contributions necessary to finance construction.                          
                                                                                    
          Stage 3: Construction and Implementation                                  
          Execution of the design, permitting, and physical development of the transit hub,
          daycare, and housing facilities.                                          
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     GLPTC July Board Meeting Pg.9                                                  
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                         Exhibit 3  
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          3. Cost Sharing                                                           
                                                                                    
          Each stage will have estimated costs, with a total not-to-exceed amount agreed upon by
          the Parties.                                                              
                                                                                    
          Stage 1: Feasibility Study: CityBus will program $80,000 of federal transit grant funds
          with a 20% matching requirement. YMCA and Ivy Tech will split matching requirement
          of $20,000 equally, $10,000 each                                          
                                                                                    
          Stage 2 and Stage 3 will be determined by addendum if all the Parties mutually agree in
          writing to proceed with the joint initiative after the feasibility study is complete.
                                                                                    
          If the actual costs exceed the estimate, the Parties agree to meet and mutually
          determine an equitable solution, which may include equal cost sharing or seeking
                                                                                    
          additional external funding.                                              
          4. Project Management                                                     
                                                                                    
            •  A Steering Committee shall be formed with at least one representative from
                                                                                    
               each Party.                                                          
            •  The Steering Committee will manage scope, budget, timeline, and strategic
               direction.                                                           
            •  Major decisions (e.g., budget changes, consultant selection) require unanimous
                                                                                    
               approval.                                                            
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          5. Term and Termination                                                   
          This Agreement will remain in effect until completion of the feasibility study, unless
                                                                                    
          earlier terminated as provided herein. If all the Parties mutually agree to proceed with
          the joint initiative after the feasibility study is complete, this Agreement may be renewed,
          revised, or amended by mutual written agreement of the Parties. The Parties can
          mutually agree to terminate this Agreement at any time for any reason. Any Party can
                                                                                    
          withdraw from this Agreement at any time for any reason. If one Party withdraws prior to
          completion, it remains responsible for its share of the costs incurred up to the date of
          withdrawal.                                                               
                                                                                    
          6. General Provisions                                                     
                                                                                    
            •  Amendments: Any amendments to this Agreement must be made in writing and
               signed by all Parties.                                               
                                                                                    
            •  Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of
               Indiana.

                CEO   REPORT   TO  GLPTC   BOARD   OF  DIRECTORS                    
                                                                                    
                              MEETING DATE: JULY 23, 2025                           
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          CEO                                                                       
          At the halfway point in the year, I would like to take a moment to acknowledge the absolutely
          amazing group of people I have the honor to work with here at CityBus. In the first six months
          of the year, we have accomplished so much. From a clean fiscal audit, multiple grant
          applications, preparing for a triennial review to redesigning the route system, fare structure, and
          onboarding 35 new hires since the start of the year. Our operators and technicians have
          stepped up as well, learning the new route structure, bringing old buses back to life, and
          clearing out the boneyard for construction of the new hydrogen fueling station. I feel incredibly
          fortunate to work with all of them.                                       
          The second half of the year holds just as busy of a schedule, as we launch the new system
          redesign, a new electronic fare collection system, and prepare for the delivery of our first
          hydrogen fuel cell buses. All of this while we are also working closely with our partners at the
          Cities and County on funding our budget for 2026.                         
          We have the framework of an agreement with Purdue for off-campus access to our fixed routes
          this fall, and I will have more information at the Board meeting.         
          COO                                                                       
          The last month has been focused on critical preparation as we finalize plans for the launch of
          the Reimagine CityBus route network, following the Board’s approval of the plan. The new
          network is scheduled to go live on Monday, August 11th. Staff across departments have been
          focused on ensuring a smooth and successful rollout for both riders and employees.
                                                                                    
          We are developing a launch plan that includes updates to the website, preparation of a printed
          rider guide, and the deployment of trained ambassadors at key transfer points, particularly at the
          CityBus Center and Ivy Tech. These ambassadors will support riders during the transition and
          help foster early confidence in the new network.                          
                                                                                    
          As part of this effort, we have also been communicating directly with demand response riders
          regarding how the network changes will affect ACCESS paratransit trip availability, which
          mirrors the new fixed-route network. In addition, we are notifying riders about the upcoming
          discontinuation of FLEX service, ending on Friday, August 8th.            
                                                                                    
          In Operations, we have seen several administrative staffing changes. One supervisor accepted
          a lateral move into a new role, a dispatcher was promoted to supervisor, and two dispatcher
          positions are currently open. Recruitment efforts are underway to maintain effective service
          oversight and control.                                                    
                                                                                    
          Coordinating with the CEO and CFO to continue the budget development process for the
          upcoming fiscal year. Our focus remains on allocating limited resources strategically to preserve
          service and meet long-term goals.                                         
                                                                                    
          We are also continuing discussions with Purdue University to explore ways to support affiliated
          riders’ access to our fixed-route network, especially for those who rely on transit for daily travel
          beyond the core campus area.                                              
          Finally, coordinated across divisions to successfully submit this year’s Low-No grant application
