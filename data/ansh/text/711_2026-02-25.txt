     We have conducted a procurement for a consultant to provide a feasibility study for a transfer center at
     Ivy Tech. As a reminder, this is a joint project with Ivy Tech and the Greater Lafayette YMCA, where
     they are each paying half of the local share for the study ($20,000 of the $100,000 total).
                                                                                    
     We received a single proposal from Etica Group (attached) to conduct the study. Pursuant to our
     Procurement Procedures, we have determined that there was sufficient competition with three
                                                                                    
     companies requesting the proposal documents and then declining to bid.         
                                                                                    
     The project and price proposal has been reviewed by me, Aaron Baute from Ivy Tech, and Paul Cramer
     from YMCA. It is a fair and reasonable price for the tasks involved. The project timeline is 15 weeks
     from kickoff.                                                                  
                                                                                    
     I request the Board authorize me to finalize and execute the contract with legal counsel.

     TO:       Board of Directors                                                   
                                                                                    
     FROM:     Bryan D. Smith, CEO                                                  
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     DATE:     February 20, 2026                                                    
                                                                                    
     RE:       Hydrogen Fuel Station Change Order                                   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     The project team for the hydrogen fueling station has experienced a setback in the delivery date for the
     compressor. This is a major part of the system and Buy America compliant sources are limited
     nationwide. The compressor manufacturer moved facilities and delayed delivery from March to June,
     after an initial delay from November to March. As part of the Build Operate Transfer (BOT)
     procurement, the costs of this delay have fallen to the project team, not CityBus. We have negotiated
     this change order as compensation for the delay from November to March. We are still negotiating
     compensation for the second delay.                                             
                                                                                    
     This change order is for the addition of a secondary delivery method for hydrogen to the dispenser unit.
     This method would allow CityBus to directly connect a high-pressure hydrogen trailer, bypassing the
     compressor. The project team had proposed this originally as an option for the initial contract as a
     backup, should the compressor fail for an extended period as a way to fuel buses while it was repaired
     or replaced. We declined for budget reasons, figuring that having four buses in the fleet down would
                                                                                    
     not affect service, and that we could add the second method at a later date.   
                                                                                    
     Once the project team was notified of the delay from the compressor manufacturer, they focused on
     how to ensure CityBus could fuel the buses when they were delivered. Installing this second method
     was the obvious choice. The project team has agreed to pay the cost of the materials for the install,
     and we would agree to pay the additional design and labor for its install. In my opinion, this is a
     reasonable compromise that delivers CityBus a backup method of delivery in perpetuity as a discounted
     cost.                                                                          
                                                                                    
     The cost share from the project team is commensurate with an estimated cost of delay. I recommend
     the Board grant me authority to execute the change order.

       SCOPE  OF SERVICES

           runs from the TTIP to MRF (1) run of Medium Pressure SS Tubing and (6) runs of
           Instrument Air Lines per the redlined P&ID (not part of Original Scope for project 2463-
           4050-50). This tubing to stay in place as a primary service line for the TTIP to MRF and
           will remain in place for backup to the Original Design tubing route of Trailer Stanchion to
           Compressor/Hydrogen Storage/Dispense Valve Panel per original project scope for 2463-
           4050-50.                                                                 
                                                                                    
                  MP Tubing Spec - 9/16" MP - 0.312"ID - 316SS Cone and Threaded Tubing
                  Instrument Air Tubing Spec - 3/8" OD UV‑Rated Poly Tubing with Compression fittings

             B.Provide and Install MP Tubing per the above specifications           
                                                                                    
             C.Provide and Install Instrument Air Tubing Spec per the above specifications
             D.All tubing to be mounted to tube supports. (Tube supports installation during DLZ Project Scope 2463-
             4050-50)                                                               
                                                                                    
             E.Hydrogen High Pressure Stanchion, all valving and specialty equipment to be provided by others.
             F.This proposal includes 16 man hours support for commissioning and leak/pressure check for
             equipment in this scope of work. All test gasses provided by others. No other NDE/NDT is included.

     RE:       Employee Handbook Update                                             
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     Dusty Sturgeon has requested to update the CityBus Employee Handbooks and consolidate
     procedures from several sources into one authoritative document (see attached memo).
                                                                                    
     In the past, the Board has voted on approval for sections of the handbook while more mundane issues
     have been dealt with at a staff level. This has created a confusing mix of Board approved policies with
     day-to-day operational changes. As part of this update, I am asking the Board to consider creating a
                                                                                    
     Personnel Policy that would contain the overarching principles for staff, along with the mandatory legal
     policies like EEO and ADA. This would mirror the process we followed in 2023 with the Procurement
     Policy, where the Board established the policy with direction to the CEO to create procedures consistent
     with the Policy. This allows staff the flexibility to update procedures based on operational need without
     asking the Board to wade into the minutia of the day-to-day functions.         
                                                                                    
     With Board approval, staff will develop a handbook consistent with the Personnel Policy.

     TO:       Bryan D. Smith                                                       
                                                                                    
     FROM:     Dusty Sturgeon, Human Resources Administrator                        
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     DATE:     February 20, 2026                                                    
                                                                                    
     RE:       Proposal to Modernize and Merge Employee Handbooks                   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     The Administrative Employee Handbook (last updated in 2018) and the Bargaining Unit Employee
     Handbook (last updated in 2014) are significantly outdated and no longer reflect current laws,
     workplace standards, or CityBus operational practices. Both handbooks contain conflicting language,
     obsolete procedures, and gaps that create confusion for employees and supervisors.
                                                                                    
     Merging the two into a single, modernized handbook will provide one authoritative, up-to-date
     resource while affirming that the Collective Bargaining Agreement continues to govern all terms and
     conditions for bargaining unit employees, with the handbook applying only where the CBA is silent.
                                                                                    
     A full rewrite will allow HR to update the structure, tone, and content; remove outdated or inaccurate
     language; and ensure clarity, consistency, and legal defensibility.            
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     With approval, HR will begin a structured review and drafting process, followed by leadership and
     union review.

                CEO   REPORT   TO  GLPTC   BOARD   OF  DIRECTORS                    
                                                                                    
                             MEETING DATE: February 25, 2026                        
                                                                                    
          CEO                                                                       
          It is my pleasure to welcome Jacques Vanier to the Board. Mayor Roswarski 
          administered the Oath of Office for Jacques at City Hall on February 10th.
          It has been a busy month. I have met with both Mayors and Commissioner Murtaugh to
          update them on our route for 2026, with service reallocation and reductions to conform
          with our budget for the year. I let them know we are committed to providing the same
          level of excellent service, with a smaller footprint. We have their support as we are
          going through this period of reinvention. They have similar issues with shrinking
          revenues, as last year’s tax bill begins to take effect.                  
                                                                                    
          As we drive through these challenges, it is appropriate for CityBus to reflect on our
          Mission, Vision, and Values statements. They are somewhat lengthy, so I won’t restate
                                                                                    
          them here, but you can view them on our website. Senior staff spent some time during
