            PURPOSE                                                                 
                                                                                    
            This Policy establishes which employment-related policies and procedures require Board of
            Directors approval and which are delegated to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO), to ensure
            clear governance, efficient administration, compliance with federal transit and federal
            contractor requirements, and consistency with the Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA)
            between GLPTC and Local No. 1741 of the Amalgamated Transit Union.

            policy in the following categories:                                     
                                                                                    
            A. Corporate   Compliance   and  Anti-Discrimination                    
                                                                                    
               1. Equal Employment Opportunity Policy – Board-level commitment to non-
                 discrimination in all employment practices, recruitment, hiring, compensation,
                 benefits, promotions, and termination, regardless of race, color, religion, sex, sexual
                 orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, or other legally protected
                 status.                                                            
               2. Anti-Harassment and Retaliation Prevention Policy – Including sexual
                 harassment, race/color/religion/gender/national origin/age/disability-based
                 harassment, and retaliation for reporting violations or participating in investigations.
                 This policy framework must be consistent with Title VII of the Civil Rights Act, as
                 amended, and all applicable state and local laws.                  
                                                                                    
               3. Drug-Free Workplace Policy and FTA Anti-Drug and Alcohol Misuse Prevention
                 Plan Framework – Board-level adoption of the corporation's commitment to
                 compliance with 49 CFR Parts 40 and 655 (Federal Transit Administration

               5. Leave Administration Framework – Board-approved policies regarding Family and

            III. UNIFIED     HANDBOOK          STRUCTURE         AND                
                                                                                    
            RELATIONSHIP           TO  THE    CBA                                   
                                                                                    
            The corporation maintains one unified employee handbook applicable to all employees. The
            handbook shall clearly identify which provisions apply to bargaining-unit employees, which
            apply to non-bargaining employees, and which apply to all employees, as follows:
                                                                                    
               1. For Bargaining-Unit Employees: The Collective Bargaining Agreement with ATU
                 Local 1741 controls all terms and conditions of employment. Any handbook section
                 addressing bargaining-unit employees shall be explicitly labeled as such and shall
                 direct employees to the CBA for the governing terms. Handbook content shall not be
                 interpreted to modify, expand, or limit rights or obligations established by the CBA.
                 Where handbook language and CBA language differ or appear to conflict, the CBA
                 controls.                                                          
               2. For Non-Bargaining Employees: The unified handbook provides guidance on
                 policies and procedures applicable to non-bargaining employees. The handbook does
                 not constitute an employment contract, and employment remains at-will. The CEO
                 retains authority to revise, supplement, or rescind handbook policies for non-
                 bargaining employees consistent with this Board Policy and applicable law.
                                                                                    
               3. For All Employees: The handbook shall include policies governing equal
                 employment opportunity, anti-harassment, anti-retaliation, workplace safety, and
                 other legally mandated protections applicable to all employees regardless of
                 classification.                                                    
               4. Clarity and Cross-References: The handbook shall use consistent labeling (e.g.,
                 "Bargaining-Unit Employees," "Non-Bargaining Employees," or "All Employees") and
                 shall include a guide or table in the front matter directing employees to the sections
                 that apply to their classification. For any topic governed by the CBA (such as wages,
                 benefits, leave, or discipline), the handbook shall cite the relevant CBA section and
                 advise bargaining-unit employees to consult the CBA for complete terms.
               5. Interpretation: Any ambiguity between handbook language and the CBA shall be
                 resolved in favor of the CBA.

            IV.  CONSTRAINTS          AND    SAFEGUARDS                             
                                                                                    
            The CEO's delegated authority is conditioned on the following:          
                                                                                    
               1. All policies adopted or revised shall comply with applicable federal, state, and local
                 law, including but not limited to Title VII of the Civil Rights Act, Americans with
                 Disabilities Act, Family and Medical Leave Act, Fair Labor Standards Act,
                 Occupational Safety and Health Act, Federal Transit Administration regulations, and
                 all other applicable statutes and regulations.                     
               2. For bargaining-unit employees, all policies shall be consistent with the CBA and shall
                 not unilaterally modify or supplement CBA terms.                   
                                                                                    
               3. No policy adopted by the CEO shall commit the corporation to financial obligations
                 materially exceeding those approved in the board-approved annual budget without
                 prior board approval.                                              
               4. All employee handbooks and policies adopted by the CEO shall include clear
                 disclaimers regarding their non-contractual nature and the corporation's right to
                 modify them.                                                       
                                                                                    
               5. The CEO shall communicate promptly to the Board any policy changes that materially
                 affect legal compliance, create new financial exposure, or respond to regulatory
                 change.                                                            
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            V.  REPORTING         TO   THE   BOARD                                  
                                                                                    
            The CEO shall provide the Board with:                                   
                                                                                    
               1. Annual Summary Report: A brief written summary of significant HR policy changes,
                 new handbooks or handbook revisions, and updates on the corporation's compliance
                 status with respect to federal contractor obligations, including equal employment
                 opportunity, anti-harassment, FTA anti-drug and alcohol, ADA accommodation, and
                 other required compliance areas.                                   
               2. Prompt Notice of Material Changes: Prompt written notification to the Board Chair
                 (or Board) of any policy change or legal development that materially affects the
                 corporation's legal obligations or creates new financial or operational risk.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            VI.  REVIEW      AND    AMENDMENT                                       
                                                                                    
            This Board Policy shall be reviewed and reaffirmed or amended as needed by the Board of
            Directors, but no less frequently than every three (3) years, or upon execution of a new
            Collective Bargaining Agreement.
