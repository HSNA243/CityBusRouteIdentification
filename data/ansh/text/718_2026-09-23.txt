          Mr. Ben Murray made the motion to approve the 2027 Budget. Ms. Julie Ginn seconded

          Mr. Bryan Smith discussed several line items in the 2027 budget exhibit. The
          organization is currently identifying additional sources of revenue, and the approach
          taken appears promising. The goal is to generate additional revenue while minimizing
                                                                                    
          the need for budget reductions.                                           
                                                                                    
          Operator’s wages were reduced due to a decrease in service hours and the amount of
          service currently being provided on the road. CityBus does not currently have a Chief
          Financial Officer (CFO), so the budget includes funding at the higher end of the
          anticipated salary range for this position. Mr. Smith also reviewed several significant
          budget line items and incorporated additional funding to allow for potential inflationary
          increases. Overall, the proposed approach is intended to strengthen the budget while
          providing a more sustainable financial outlook for 2027.

     DATE:     September 22, 2026                                                   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     RE:       Kloepher Consulting Contract                                         
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     In January of this year, CityBus hired Kloepher Consulting for a short-term contract to assist with grants
     management. This additional help was needed to assist the Finance Department for several reasons,
     including additional internal controls, increased FTA requirements, and the planned implementation of
     new financial software (NetSuite).                                             
                                                                                    
     Riley Kloepfer of Kloepher Consulting, serves as CityBus's grant management consultant, providing
     end-to-end administration of federal and state transit grants. His responsibilities include grant
     development and submission, federal reporting, grant amendments, compliance monitoring,
     drawdowns, and coordination with FTA, INDOT, and regional planning agencies. Riley also supports
     capital planning, fleet replacement strategies, budgeting, and preparation for federal oversight reviews.
     Through these efforts, he helps CityBus maximize funding opportunities, maintain compliance, and
     successfully implement major capital and operating projects.                   
                                                                                    
     Mr. Kloepher is a Lafayette native, who prior to starting his consulting firm, worked at the FTA’s Region
     V office in Chicago. He has years of experience with grant management and compliance, as well as
                                                                                    
     personal experience with the Greater Lafayette area. He does work for several transits throughout
     Indiana and the United States.                                                 
                                                                                    
     My intention was to issue an RFP in the first quarter of the year for these services but could not do so
     until recently. I signed a second contract after the first quarter, again with the intention of issuing an
     RFP in the summer. To maintain transparency, I am asking the Board to approve a third contract
     through the end of the year. The RFP responses are due November 20th, with award expected in
     December for a year-to-year, on-call contract to begin in January of 2027. This work has been included
     in the budget for 2027. There will be a not-to-exceed amount, as well as no minimum charge as part
     of that contract so the duties can be brought in-house if desired by the new CFO.
                                                                                    
     This contract has a not-to-exceed of $30,000, and with the other contracts, would be just under $90,000
     for the year, if that limit is reached. We have budget available for this expense and it is eligible for grant
     funding up to 80% of the cost.

        1.PURPOSE                                                                   
        The purpose of this Agreement is to provide professional consulting services related to the Federal Transit
        Administration (FTA) grant management, compliance, and administration for the Client.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
        2. SCOPE OF SERVICES                                                        
                                                                                    
        The Consultant shall provide services including, but not limited to:        
            • FTA grant application preparation and review (e.g., Section 5307, 5339, LoNo, etc.)
                                                                                    
            • Grant management within TrAMS and ECHO systems                        
            • Financial management and drawdown compliance                          
            • Unliquidated obligation (ULO) monitoring and reconciliation           
            • FTA Master Agreement compliance support                               
                                                                                    
            • Procurement guidance in accordance with 2 CFR Part 200 and FTA Circulars
            • Grant amendments, budget revisions, and closeout support              
            • Triennial Review and State Management Review preparation              
                                                                                    
            • Policy and procedure development                                      
            • Training and technical assistance for Client staff                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
        Additional services may be authorized via written Task Orders.

        terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.
