          Bryan and the City Bus Board of Directors,                                
                                                                                    
          As I have reflected on the last 25+ years, I have found it difficult to put into words my sincere
          thanks for the partnership between City Bus and Right Steps. I have been part of this partnership
          from the beginning, so the impact of its conclusion is very personal to me.
                                                                                    
          From the beginning, this was an innovative partnership. Bringing together the need for quality
          childcare and reliable transportation provided an important service to families and to our
          community. Over the years, Right Steps and City Bus worked together to make this partnership
                                                                                    
          successful and sustainable. Looking back, I feel an incredible sense of pride in what we were
          able to accomplish together.                                              
                                                                                    
          City Bus' continued support of the childcare centers, including maintaining the buildings and
          keeping rent at a level that a nonprofit organization could sustain, played an important role in
          making this partnership possible for so many years. That support was never something we took
          for granted.                                                              
                                                                                    
          Over the last year, Right Steps has had to make some very difficult decisions. Childcare funding
          was dramatically reduced, families lost access to funding, state support for childcare programs
          changed, and our enrollment was significantly affected. At the same time, the cost of operating
          our centers continued to rise. The decisions we faced were not easy, and closing the Wabash
          Landing and Downtown sites were among the most difficult.                 
                                                                                    
          I truly wish we could go back two years and have our partnership continue as it had for so many
                                                                                    
          years. But as many nonprofit organizations are experiencing, circumstances have required us to
          make difficult choices and rethink how we can continue serving our community.
                                                                                    
          Even though this chapter of our partnership has come to an end, I sincerely hope that it is not the
          end of our relationship. If opportunities arise in the future where City Bus and Right Steps can
          once again collaborate in ways that support children, families, and the Greater Lafayette
          community, I hope we can remain open to those possibilities.

     RE:       Adjustment to donation agreement with Lafayette Fire Department      
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     As you may recall, CityBus donated a number of vehicles, both fixed route buses and paratransit
     vehicles, that were outside of their useful life to the Lafayette Fire Department (LFD) for training
     purposes. They are using them to train firefighters on vehicle fires, evacuation, etc.
                                                                                    
     We included a provision that the vehicles would not be re-titled for use on the road as part of the
     donation agreement.                                                            
                                                                                    
     LFD has identified a need for a vehicle capable of transporting groups of firefighters, and has requested
     to be allowed to re-title one of the paratransit vehicles for that use. The vehicle would need significant
     rehab to be road-worthy, but it would still be cheaper than purchasing a new one for LFD.
                                                                                    
     I believe this is still in line with the nature of the donation, as it helps LFD with a need. The vehicle is
     no longer insured by CityBus, and no liability will attach to CityBus if LFD were to re-title it in their name.
                                                                                    
     The attached contract amendment would allow LFD to re-title the vehicle so long as the CityBus logos
                                                                                    
     (if any remain) be removed and that LFD properly register the vehicle for their use.
                                                                                    
     I request authority to execute the contract amendment as prepared by Board counsel.

       Valuation Considerations                                                     
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       Coinsurance (Co-Ins)                                                         
       A policy provision requiring the insured to carry insurance equal to a specific percentage of the value of the property
       covered. The basic principal of property insurance is most easily expressed by this formula:
                                                                                    
       (amount of insurance purchased) x (amount of loss) = (amount paid) (amount required)
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       Agreed Amount (V)                                                            
       The Agreed Amount endorsement suspends the coinsurance clause after the insurance company and the insured
       agree that the amount of insurance carried on the property is equal to at least 90% of the replacement cost of the
       property.                                                                    
                                                                                    
       Actual Cash Value (A)                                                        
       The Actual Cash Value is the current value of the damaged or destroyed property at the time of the loss less the
       depreciation of the damaged property (due to the age and condition of the property) at the time of the loss.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       Actual Loss Sustained (L)                                                    
       In the event of a covered loss which suspends your operations, if you have purchased Loss of Income coverage with
       the Actual Loss Sustained feature, the insurance company will pay the actual loss of your claim in lieu of a pre-
       determined amount.                                                           
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       Functional Replacement Cost (F)                                              
       Functional Replacement Cost is used in situations where replacing damaged or destroyed property is impractical or
       unnecessary. It affords you the ability to substitute property which is substantially different in value or cost from the
       original property. Consequently, you are allowed to carry policy limits lower than what would normally be required to
       rebuild your building.                                                       
                                                                                    
       Guaranteed Replacement Cost (G)                                              
       Guaranteed Replacement Cost pays for the cost to replace or rebuild damaged or destroyed property without a
       deduction for depreciation and without a dollar limit.                       
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       Replacement Cost (R)                                                         
       Replacement Cost is the cost to rebuild or replace damaged or destroyed property without a deduction for
       depreciation.

       Workers  Compensation Glossary of Terms                                      
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       Defense Base Act: An endorsement that provides U.S. Longshoremen and Harbor Workers coverage to the
       employees of contractors performing work at domestic and foreign military bases scheduled on the
       endorsement.                                                                 
                                                                                    
       Foreign Liability: Coverage for employees that are working in a foreign country on more than a temporary
       basis. It provides reimbursement for investigation, settlement, and defense costs incurred outside the
                                                                                    
       coverage territory and should also cover:                                    
       Endemic Disease: Any disease that is: Infectious and recognized as a public health hazard; Restricted to a
       locality or region; and is not caused by or aggravated by the conditions of your employment. This coverage
       pays for the cost of medical care for such diseases.                         
       Repatriation Expense: Pays the cost of returning the employee to the United States for treatment. It also
       pays for the repatriation of a body of an employee for burial when injury or illness has resulted in death.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       Monopolistic State: A state that does not allow competition within Workers Compensation Insurance.
       Only a single State Fund is available, and all insurance is placed with or through that State Fund. These
       states include: North Dakota, Ohio, Washington and Wyoming.                  
                                                                                    
       Second Injury Fund: A State Fund that pays permanent Workers Compensation benefits to an employee
       who is totally or permanently disabled by a work event and also has a partial disability or injury that
       preceded the work event.                                                     
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       Stop Gap Coverage: This endorsement is used to provide Employers Liability coverage for operations that
       are conducted in a monopolistic fund state. It is necessary because Workers Compensation policies written
       by these state funds do not provide Employers Liability Coverage.            
                                                                                    
       U.S. Longshoremen and Harbor Workers Coverage (USL&H): A federal act designed to provide
       compensation to an employee if an injury or death occurs upon navigable waters of the United States –
       including any pier, wharf, dry dock, terminal, building-way, marine railway or other adjoining area
                                                                                    
       customarily used by an employer in loading, unloading, repairing, dismantling or building a vessel.
                                                                                    
       Voluntary Compensation: This endorsement is issued to provide Workers Compensation coverage for
       injured employees who are not eligible for mandatory coverage or whose status is doubtful. It is important
       to note that employees covered under this act may voluntarily reject the coverage and elect instead to
       bring a lawsuit against the employer.                                        
                                                                                    
       Waiver of Subrogation: A waiver by the Named Insured giving up any right of recovery against another
                                                                                    
       party.

The coverages described above are not automatically included in your policy.
